# Opus 5 is FINALLY here! (WOAH)

[![Opus 5 is FINALLY here! (WOAH)](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/tHQ34j8_toI/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI)

## TL;DR
Anthropic has released Claude Opus 5, and it surprisingly outperforms the larger, more expensive Fable 5 model on nearly every benchmark while costing half the price. The model shows major improvements in coding, agentic tasks, and novel problem-solving (ARC AGI 3), with intentionally reduced cybersecurity capabilities that paradoxically don't hurt overall performance.

## Key Takeaways
- Opus 5 beats Fable 5 on almost every benchmark despite being half the price ($5/M input, $25/M output — same as Opus 4.8)
- ARC AGI 3 score jumped from ~8% (previous best) to 30%, a massive leap in novel problem-solving
- Cost-per-task is the metric that matters, not cost-per-token; Opus 5 excels here, outperforming GPT 5.6 Soul at roughly half the cost
- Cybersecurity exploit development was intentionally reduced (exploitation success dropped from 13 with Mythos to 4 with Opus 5), yet overall model quality improved — unusual when adding guardrails
- The model includes automatic API fallbacks to Opus 4.8 when safety classifiers flag requests, which is already being observed in practice
- Box's enterprise benchmarks showed sizable improvements over Opus 4.8, particularly in due diligence (65→76) and data analysis (+6 points)
- Three models now sit at the Pareto frontier of quality and cost: Opus 5, GPT 5.6 Soul, and the open-source Kimmy K3
- At the highest thinking level on Frontier Bench, the score actually went down and cost more — suggesting diminishing returns
- The host speculates the U.S. government likely reviewed the model before release, which may explain why it's expected to stay available

### Introduction and Benchmark Surprise [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=0s)
The video opens with the announcement of Claude Opus 5, part of Anthropic's model family (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus). ### Benchmark Highlights [00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=31s)
Key benchmark results are walked through: agentic terminal coding (43 vs. 33 on Frontier Bench), a 100-point improvement on GPQA Val, ARC AGI 3 at 30% (a massive jump), Browse Comp at 90% vs. 87%, and OS World (computer use) with a 4-point improvement. Deep SUI, which the host considers the best vibe-check benchmark, showed only a slight drop. Legal and HealthBench Professional scores saw minor decreases.

### Cost Per Task Analysis [02:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=159s)
The host emphasizes that cost-per-task — not cost-per-token — is the critical metric. A chart shows Opus 5 scoring higher on OS World than both Fable 5 and Opus 4.8 while costing less. GPT 5.6 Soul requires over twice the cost to match Opus 5's performance at its lowest setting. The host references Kimmy K3 as a cautionary example: half the token price but twice the token usage, making it a wash.

### Box Sponsorship and Enterprise Benchmarks [04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=284s)
Sponsor Box tested Opus 5 across 12 industries on realistic document-grounded tasks. Results showed a jump from 63 to 78 on one benchmark, due diligence from 65 to 76, and a 6-point data analysis improvement. The host praises Box's CLI integration with his own agents at Forward Future.

### Pricing and Thinking Levels [06:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=379s)
Opus 5 is priced at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens — identical to Opus 4.8 and half of Fable's price. On Humanity's Last Exam, pricing is comparable across thinking levels. On Frontier Bench, spending ~$2 more per task yields ~5% more, but the highest thinking level actually produced a lower score at higher cost.

### ARC AGI 3 and Cybersecurity [07:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=471s)
The ARC AGI 3 benchmark drops models into games with no instructions, requiring pure novel problem-solving. Opus 5 hit 30%, more than tripling the previous best of ~8%. On cybersecurity, Opus 5 scores an exploitation success rate of 4, compared to Mythos 5 at 13 and Opus 4.8 at 0. The host speculates Anthropic intentionally reduced cyber capabilities while improving everything else — a rare feat when adding guardrails.

### Blog Details and Availability [09:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=569s)
The blog confirms Opus 5 outperforms Fable 5 on almost everything except cybersecurity. A demo shows Opus 5 building a working wind tunnel simulation. The model is available today on all platforms. The host notes GPT 5.6 Soul is likely the last GPT-5 iteration before OpenAI moves to GPT-6.

### Safety Fallbacks and Government Speculation [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=600s)
Automatic API fallbacks route flagged requests to Opus 4.8, and users pay the fallback model's price. Users are already reporting fallbacks. The host speculates the government likely reviewed the model pre-release, and the intentional cybersecurity guardrails may be why Opus 5 avoids being taken offline.

### Turk's Commentary and Pareto Frontier [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHQ34j8_toI&t=723s)
Turk (likely an Anthropic executive) describes Opus 5 as rounding out the Claude 5 family and recommends pairing it with Fable for planning and hard bugs. The host finds the "pair it with Fable" suggestion interesting. Three models now define the Pareto frontier of quality and cost: Opus 5, GPT 5.6 Soul, and open-source Kimmy K3.
