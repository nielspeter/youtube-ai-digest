## TL;DR
This video demonstrates ChatGPT's Voice feature as a persistent, hands-free work companion that maintains an ongoing conversation while you move between apps. Unlike simple dictation, Voice can see your screen, pull context from connected tools like Slack and Gmail, and execute multi-step tasks in the background—letting you stay in your workflow without stopping.

## Key Takeaways
- **Voice vs. Dictation**: Dictation simply converts speech into a text prompt; Voice is a continuous conversation that stays with you as you work across apps.
- Voice can see your screen context when asked, allowing it to reference what you're viewing in real time.
- It pulls information from connected apps (Slack, Gmail, calendar, Navan) without requiring you to navigate to each one manually.
- You can start Voice from the real-time voice button or a hotkey, and it persists as you switch between applications.
- Voice handles background tasks—such as preparing a pitch deck or staging travel bookings—while you continue other work.
- When a background task completes, Voice brings it back to your attention for review, redirection, or next steps.
- It can stage actions (like flight and hotel bookings in Navan) up to the final review step without completing purchases.
- Voice enables a single ongoing conversation to manage multiple workstreams simultaneously, keeping you "in flow."
