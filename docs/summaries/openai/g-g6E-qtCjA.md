- Reports include **trends over time**, an executive summary, objection breakdowns by category, and competitor presence in deals.
- The system surfaces **operational opportunities**, such as areas to shift resources based on customer feedback.
- A key focus is identifying **what top-performing reps do differently** and using those behaviors to up-level the rest of the team.

- The demo uses a fictional company, **Blossom Systems**, launching a product called **Blossom Observability**.
