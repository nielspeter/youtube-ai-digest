### Advice for Legacy Enterprises [00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oXu6JGxW_YM&t=30s)
Williams pivots to offering guidance for people working in enterprise or legacy companies. He candidly warns that the journey will be bumpy, with a lot to do and a lot to change.
